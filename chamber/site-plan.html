Site Plan

Site Name

São Paulo Chamber of Commerce

This name represents a chamber that connects and promotes local businesses in São Paulo, Brazil.

Site Purpose

  • List member businesses in a searchable directory.
  • Share local events and today's weather.
  • Explain membership levels and let new businesses join with an online form.

Scenarios

  • How can my business join the chamber and what are the benefits?
  • Where can I find the contact information of local businesses?
  • What events are happening in the city this month?

Color Scheme

  • Deep blue #0B4F6C: header, footer, headings and buttons.
  • Dark slate #1B2A33: body text.
  • Light gray-blue #EEF2F4: page background.
  • Burnt orange #B7410E: accents and call-to-action buttons.

Typography

  • Roboto Slab: headings.
  • Roboto: body text, menu and forms.
