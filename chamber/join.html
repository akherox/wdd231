NP Membership

Free. For non-profit organizations.

  • Listing in the online member directory
  • Invitations to community networking events
  • Monthly chamber newsletter

Bronze Membership

R$ 150 / year. A simple start for small businesses.

  • Everything in NP Membership
  • Business profile with logo in the directory
  • Discounts on chamber events
  • Access to one training workshop per year

Silver Membership

R$ 400 / year. More visibility and more training.

  • Everything in Bronze Membership
  • Advertising in the monthly newsletter
  • Access to all training workshops
  • Larger discounts on chamber events

Gold Membership

R$ 900 / year. Our top level, with the best visibility.

  • Everything in Silver Membership
  • Spotlight position on the chamber home page
  • Free booth at the Holiday Shop Local Fair
  • Priority invitations to special events
